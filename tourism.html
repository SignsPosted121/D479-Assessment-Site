Taniti

Tourism

What to do

Explore the Island!

  • Visiting beaches
  • Rainforest tours (with zip-lines!)
  • Volcano tours
  • Snorkeling
  • Bus tours
  • Helicopter rides
  • Chartered fishing tours

Explore the City!

  • Local history museum
  • Bar-crawling several pubs (including a microbrewery!)
  • Dance club (just opened!)
  • Movie theater
  • Local arcade
  • Multiple art galleries
  • Bowling gallery
  • 9-hole Golf Course (coming next year!)

Many of these activities can be found in Merriton Landing, which is a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay!

Getting To The Island

Almost all visitors arrive to Taniti by air, though some arrive on a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week. Taniti is served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. Taniti is in the process of expanding the airport so larger jets will be able to land on the island within the next few years.

Lodging

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. There are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts. All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Groceries & Restaurants

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day. Currently there are 10 restaurants on the island: five serve mostly local fish and rice, three serve American-style meals, and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

Transportation

Public

  • Taniti City Buses (from 5AM to 11PM daily).
  • Taniti City Taxis.
  • Island-wide rideshares
  • Island-wide private bus routes

Personal

  • Bike and hemlet rentals (helmets are required by law).
  • Rental car agency (near airport)

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. Many tourists stay in the area surrounding Merriton Landing: this area is easy to explore on foot.
